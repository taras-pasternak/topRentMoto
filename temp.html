    Rent Ducati Scrambler in Italy

    ‹›

    You don’t have to be a fashionable hipster to enjoy the Scrambler: its qualities shine through without clever marketing tricks. It’s easy to ride, small and light, has a lovely useable air-cooled V-twin with lots of low to mid-range power. There’s some lovely attention to detail on all the models.
    By renting a Ducati Scrambler you will be able to enjoy it's high level features:

    • A 803cc, air-cooled, motor based on the old 796 Monster unit. The bore and stroke is the same, but valve overlap, injectors, air-box and exhaust are all new. This results in 75bhp and 50.2ftlb of torque, slightly less than the old Monster, but with a greater spread of torque;
    • The Scrambler can hold its head high and punch well above its weight in handling terms. It’s relatively light and nimble and the wide bars enable you to throw it around with ease;
    • The level of finish is very high, with some lovely attention to detail. Ducati have also worked in partnership with reliable, quality brands such as Brembo, Pirelli and Kayaba.

    The moto insurance system (CASCO) which is applied to all vehicles provides collision damage to the rented vehicle, but does not cover such things as damages to other vehicles, property or injury to other persons.
