The KTM 1190 Adventure is a motorcycle radically different from the 990. The whole combo of chassis, electronic suspension and rider aids makes this 150 hp, 230kg bike seriously capable with more rider-friendly characteristics than any previous KTM Adventure model. It can also be ridden faster: it’s similar to a Ducati Multistrada, but with much better off-road suitability.

By renting a KTM 1190 Adventure you will be able to enjoy it's high level features:

  • With 150bhp and 92ftlb of torque and a fully-fuelled weight of 230kg, the KTM has a better power-to-weight ratio than most other motorcycles in its range;
  • KTM build bikes to take on Dakar, they are rough, tough and reliable;
  • The retention of a conventional chain instead of a power-sapping shaft drive means the bike reacts far more accurately to the rider’s throttle inputs;
